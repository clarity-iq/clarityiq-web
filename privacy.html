Privacy Policy

ClarityIQ  ·  Effective date: July 24, 2026  ·  Operated by DLP Ventures, LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“we,” “us,” or “ClarityIQ”)

1. Our Approach

ClarityIQ is built to collect and retain as little personal information as possible. Conversation content is processed in real time only to generate a one-time educational summary. We do not retain the conversation, the summary, or any related content after the session ends.

2. What We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly: email address (when you request free guides or purchase a paid session).
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment provider. We do not directly collect or store card details.
  • Transient conversation content: during a paid session, the content of your conversation is processed in real time solely to generate a personalized educational summary. This content is not retained after the session ends.
  • Anonymous usage information: limited technical data about how sessions are used, collected in a form that does not identify you.

3. What We Do Not Collect or Retain

We do not build or maintain a health record or profile tied to your identity. We do not retain audio, transcripts, conversation content, or the generated summary after the session ends and the one-time email has been sent. Our email providers are configured so that message-body content is not retained after successful delivery.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and deliver the ClarityIQ service (including generating and emailing your one-time summary);
  • Process payments and provide customer support;
  • Communicate with you about your purchase or free resources when appropriate;
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns so we can improve the tool over time.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use standard service providers (payment processing, conversational AI infrastructure, transactional email, and hosting) who are contractually required to protect your information and to use it only to provide their services to us. We do not share conversation content or summaries with any third party for their own purposes.

6. Retention

  • Conversation content and generated summaries: not retained after the session ends and the one-time email is sent.
  • Email address and payment records: retained as ordinary commercial records for tax, accounting, customer-support, and legal-compliance purposes.
  • Anonymous usage data: retained in aggregate form only.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may request that we delete the email address and related commercial records we hold about you by contacting us at support@clarityiq.ai. We will respond to verified requests in accordance with applicable law. Because we do not retain conversation content or summaries, there is no stored health-related content to access or delete after the session ends.

8. Washington Consumer Health Data Notice

If you are a Washington resident, please note the following: during a paid session, conversation content that relates to your health interests, concerns, or questions about cataract lens options is processed in real time to generate a one-time educational summary. This processing is limited to providing the service you requested. We do not retain this content after the session ends. We do not sell consumer health data. For questions or to exercise rights under the Washington My Health My Data Act, contact us at support@clarityiq.ai.

9. Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the limited information we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of ClarityIQ after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to support@clarityiq.ai.
