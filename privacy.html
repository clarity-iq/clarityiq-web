Privacy Policy

ClarityIQ  ·  Effective date: TBD at launch  ·  Operated by DLP Ventures, LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“we,” “us,” or “ClarityIQ”)

1. Our Approach

ClarityIQ is built to collect and keep as little personal information as possible. Conversation content is processed in real time only to generate a one-time educational summary. We do not keep the conversation, the summary, or related content after the session ends. During our free phase, we also ask a few short questions before and after a session to help us improve the service. ClarityIQ is education, not medical care, and using it does not create a doctor-patient relationship.

2. What We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly: your email address, when you request free guides, get or purchase a session, or choose to receive a follow-up message.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not collect or store card details.
  • Free access codes: a code we issue so you can start a free session.
  • Before-session questions (required to get a free code): where you are in the process (just learning, surgery planned, date set, or helping someone else), how confident you feel (0–10), what matters most to you (distance, reading, cost, night driving, or not sure), and, optionally, how you heard about us.
  • After-session questions (optional): how confident you feel (0–10), how likely you are to recommend us (0–10), what you might pay for a session (a price range), what was unclear, whether anything didn’t work, and, if you check the consent box, your email for one follow-up message.
  • Transient conversation content: during a session, free or paid, what you say is processed in real time only to generate your educational summary. It is not kept after the session ends.
  • Usage and analytics information: limited technical events about how people move through the site (for example, visiting the home page, starting a session, or starting checkout), collected through Cloudflare, including Cloudflare Web Analytics. We use this in aggregate and do not use it to build profiles of individuals.

About the survey

The survey is for education and product improvement only. Your answers are not a medical or health record, and we do not use them to give medical advice. Please don’t include medical details (diagnoses, test results, medications, or doctors’ names) in the free-text boxes.

3. What We Do Not Collect or Keep

We do not build or keep a health record or profile tied to your identity. We do not keep audio, transcripts, conversation content, or the generated summary after the session ends and the one-time email is sent. Our voice AI provider is set to keep no conversation data, and session recording is turned off.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Run and deliver ClarityIQ, including issuing free access codes and generating and emailing your one-time summary;
  • Process payments and provide support;
  • Send free resources you asked for, and, only if you agreed, one follow-up message after your session;
  • Improve the service, using survey answers and aggregate usage patterns.

Survey answers are used only to improve ClarityIQ. We do not sell them or use them for advertising. An email you give in the after-session survey is used only for the one follow-up message you agreed to.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, including survey answers. We use service providers to run ClarityIQ: payment processing (Stripe), voice conversation (ElevenLabs), generating the session summary (Anthropic), email sign-ups (MailerLite), transactional email (SendGrid), and hosting, data storage, and analytics (Cloudflare). They may use your information only to provide their services to us. We do not share conversation content, summaries, or survey answers with anyone for their own purposes.

6. Retention

  • Conversation content and summaries: not kept after the session ends and the one-time email is sent.
  • Survey answers and free access codes: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Follow-up email from the survey: deleted after the follow-up message is sent, or after 12 months if it isn’t, whichever comes first.
  • Usage and analytics events: kept in aggregate, without names or email addresses, for as long as our analytics provider keeps them.
  • Email address and payment records from purchases: kept as ordinary business records for tax, accounting, support, and legal purposes.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may ask us to delete your email address, your survey answers, and related records by contacting support@clarityiq.ai. If you agreed to a follow-up message, you can withdraw that consent at any time by replying to the message or contacting us. We will respond to verified requests as applicable law requires. Because we do not keep conversation content or summaries, there is no stored conversation to access or delete after a session ends.

8. Washington Consumer Health Data Notice

If you live in Washington: during a session, free or paid, conversation content about your interests, concerns, or questions about cataract lens options is processed in real time to generate a one-time educational summary and is not kept afterward. Some survey answers (for example, whether surgery is planned and what matters most to you) may count as consumer health data. We collect them with your consent, use them only to improve the service, keep them for up to 12 months, share them only with the service providers listed above, and never sell them. To exercise your rights under the Washington My Health My Data Act, contact support@clarityiq.ai.

9. Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the limited information we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of ClarityIQ after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to support@clarityiq.ai.
